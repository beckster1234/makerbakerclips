4. Twitch-Chat

Um Highlights zu erkennen, liest unsere Software den öffentlichen Chat ausgewählter Twitch-Kanäle über einen anonymen Zugang (ohne eigenes Twitch-Konto) mit. Zur Erkennung verwenden wir nur zusammengefasste Kennzahlen, etwa wie viele Nachrichten pro Zeitabschnitt geschrieben werden.

Zum Abstimmen der Erkennung speichern wir vorübergehend Archive des öffentlichen Chats (Nachrichten mit dem öffentlich sichtbaren Benutzernamen und Zeitstempel). Diese Archive werden nur intern dafür verwendet, nicht veröffentlicht und nicht weitergegeben. Sie werden nach 30 Tagen automatisch gelöscht. Wir erstellen keine Profile von Chat-Nutzern.

5. Inhalte der Streams und Clips

  • Clips werden aus öffentlichen Streams bzw. deren Aufzeichnungen erstellt – nur von Streamern, die uns ihre Erlaubnis gegeben haben. Jeder Clip nennt den Streamer und verlinkt den Original-Kanal.
  • Die Sprache im Clip wird auf unserem eigenen Server in Text umgewandelt (für Untertitel).
  • Um Titel und Beschreibung vorzuschlagen, wird dieser Text an einen KI-Dienst (Anthropic, USA) übermittelt.
  • Vor jeder Veröffentlichung prüft ein Mensch den Clip; dazu wird eine Vorschau in einen privaten Telegram-Chat (Telegram Messenger) gesendet.
  • Lokale Videodateien löschen wir nach dem Upload.

6. Entfernung von Clips

2. This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics or tracking services, no JavaScript and no externally loaded fonts. It is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), which processes technically necessary connection data such as your IP address (see GitHub's privacy statement). We do not collect any data through this website ourselves.

3. Use of platform APIs (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram)

Our software uses the YouTube API Services, the TikTok Content Posting API and the Instagram Graph API solely to upload videos to our own channels and afterwards to read the details of exactly those videos of ours (for example, whether the description and visibility were applied correctly). We do not access data of other users through these APIs, and we do not store or share any such data. Instagram uploads are planned but not yet active.

YouTube

Our software uses YouTube API Services. By using this service you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies.

Access tokens (OAuth)

The access tokens for our own accounts are stored only on our own server, in files readable only by the service itself. They are used only for uploads to our channels and are never shared with third parties. Access can be revoked at any time – for Google/YouTube at myaccount.google.com/permissions, for TikTok and Instagram in the app permissions of the respective account. After a revocation we delete the stored tokens.

4. Twitch chat

To detect highlights, our software reads the public chat of selected Twitch channels through an anonymous connection (without a Twitch account). Detection uses aggregated metrics only, such as how many messages are written per time interval.

To tune the detection, we temporarily keep archives of the public chat (messages with the publicly visible username and timestamp). These archives are used internally for this purpose only and are never published or shared. They are deleted automatically after 30 days. We do not build profiles of chat users.

5. Stream content and clips

  • Clips are made from public streams or their recordings – only of streamers who have given us their permission. Every clip credits the streamer and links to the original channel.
  • Speech in a clip is transcribed on our own server (for subtitles).
  • To suggest a title and description, this text is sent to an AI service (Anthropic, USA).
  • A human reviews every clip before it is published; for that, a preview is sent to a private Telegram chat.
  • Local video files are deleted after the upload.

6. Removal of clips

Streamers can request the removal of any clip at any time by email to shorts.rb2000@gmail.com. We remove it within 48 hours.

7. Sharing and transfers abroad

We do not sell any data. Data only goes to the services named above (YouTube/Google, TikTok, Anthropic, Telegram, GitHub) as far as needed for the respective purpose. Some of these providers are based in the USA or other countries outside Switzerland.

8. Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics tools.

Under the nDSG you have the right to access, rectify and delete your data. People in the EU/EEA additionally have, under the GDPR, the rights to restriction of processing, data portability, objection and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact shorts.rb2000@gmail.com.
